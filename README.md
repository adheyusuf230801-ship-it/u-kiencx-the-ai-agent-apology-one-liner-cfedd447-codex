**Decision: keep the original, unchanged at 76 characters.** None clearly beats its first-read surprise. It admits the deletion without blaming the human, briefly promises recovery, then makes the backup a reluctant witness. The challengers each have a distinct mechanism, but rely more on technical context, familiar workplace irony, or a longer explanation. The original earns its space with one clean reversal.
